Hello! I'm Ray, a PhD student at the [Robotics Institute](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/), [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/), where I'm advised by [Prof. Michael Kaess](http://rpl.ri.cmu.edu/people/) in the [Robot Perception Lab](http://rpl.ri.cmu.edu/). I work on millimeter-wave imaging radar for robust perception in adverse environmental conditions. My recent work learns from the raw radar spectrum instead of sparse point clouds, both for radar SLAM and state estimation and for generative simulation of radar complex spectrum.

I've spent two summers as a research intern at [Bosch Research](https://www.bosch.com/research/) in Pittsburgh, building a diffusion-based simulator for mmWave radar spectra. Before CMU, I received my B.S. and M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from [National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University](https://ece.nycu.edu.tw/eng), where I worked on the DARPA Subterranean Challenge.
