Hello! I'm Ray, a PhD student at the [Robotics Institute](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/), [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/), where I'm advised by [Prof. Michael Kaess](http://rpl.ri.cmu.edu/people/). My research focuses on the intersection of robotics, SLAM, and machine learning, with particular emphasis on state estimation, sensor fusion, and autonomous navigation in challenging environments. Currently, I'm developing millimeter-wave radar-based odometry and mapping systems to enable robust perception in adverse environmental conditions.

Before my study at CMU, I graduated from [National Chiao Tung University](https://ece.nycu.edu.tw/eng) where I received my B.S. and a fifth-year M.S. degree. I was advised by [Prof. Hsueh-Cheng Wang](https://arg-nctu.github.io/research.html) and worked on the DARPA SubT Challenge during my time at NCTU. I'm also fortunate to have visited [Prof. Jenq-Neng Hwang](https://ipl-uw.github.io/)'s research group at the [University of Washington](https://www.ece.uw.edu/) and worked on multi-object tracking for autonomous driving using millimeter-wave radar.
